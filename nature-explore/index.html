02 / Our planet

Nature is not a place to visit. It is home.

From ancient forests to high mountain trails, the natural world sustains every form of life. Exploring it comes with a responsibility: leave no trace, respect wildlife and help protect the places that make our planet beautiful.

Travel gently. Carry your waste back. Stay on marked trails. Let wild animals remain wild.
